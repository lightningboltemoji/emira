# The close at the end of the strip snapped

> Change `1785112547` — 2026-07-26

## Goal

Closing a window at the far right of the strip moved every other window without animating, while a
close at the left or in the middle animated. Reported from six Ghostty windows on a two-screen-wide
strip.

## Implementation

`finishStructuralEdit` decided whether an edit warranted a cover by counting displacements. A departure
at the end of the strip displaces nobody: every survivor keeps its strip position, and the only change
is that the strip is now a column shorter than the offset the viewport rests at. `offsetToReveal`
clamps, so the destination was already correct — the guard classified the motion as invisible and
snapped to it. It now also compares the viewport (`end != start`).

The displacements are measured at a fixed offset on both sides of the edit so the scroll cancels out of
them (`StructuralSnapshot`). A scroll therefore cannot appear as a displacement, and "nothing
displaced" is not the same claim as "nothing moves". Every other departure displaced something
incidentally, which hid this.

## Observations

- Inverse of the post-close focus defect fixed earlier: that was a placeholder hidden by a snap; this
  is a snap hidden by the other closes animating.
- Both are only reachable from the far end of a strip wider than the screen.
- Regression: `GhostWindowTests.closingTheLastColumnScrollsBackInMotion` — the transition opens, starts
  from the offset the viewport had, and settles on the clamp. Headless only.
