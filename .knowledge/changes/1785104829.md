# Park a corner instead of an edge

> Change `1785104829` — 2026-07-26

## Goal

A parked column was pushed off the left edge at full height, leaving a 1 pt wide, screen-tall sliver
down the display edge. Reduce what stays on screen, and leave a part of the window that can be dragged.

## Implementation

A park now goes off two edges. The visible remainder is the window's top-left corner held against the
working area's bottom-right.

- **~40 pt tall rather than full height.** A park needs only enough visible pixels to be honored and to
  keep the window unoccluded. Losing the full-height edge removes what made successive slots distinct,
  so distinctness moves to the nub's height: `ParkingLot.stagger` is 8 pt, which must clear the ±2 pt
  per-edge tolerance `WindowRegistry.bind` matches frames within. Two parked windows of one app closer
  than that are ambiguous at cold-start rebind, and an ambiguous match is refused, so both are lost.
- **Right edge rather than left.** A user dragging a parked window back throws the pointer at the
  screen edge, where it stops. Against the right edge that lands on the window's top-left corner —
  title bar. Against the left it lands on the window's far edge, which is whatever control the app put
  there.

## Observations

- A 1 pt wide tail of the window crosses the Dock band on its way off the bottom of the display. That
  region is not under the cover.
- The lane wrap is unchanged in shape but steps by nub height rather than position; a lane holds as
  many rows as fit before the nub runs past the working area's top.
- Nothing outside `Park.swift` and the overlay's inset changed. A park slot is target geometry, so the
  reducer, the sweep and the capture scope picked it up unchanged.
