# Veil a sheet with its window

> Change `1789617270` — 2026-09-16

## Goal

Two Ghostty windows at half width, `echo hello | less` in one, ⌘W on it, then focus the other. The "Close
Window?" sheet stayed solid on the veiled window, as intended, but the solid patch was its square frame. The
sheet's corners are rounded, so the window under each corner showed unveiled in four sharp triangles, and
the patch did not trace the sheet.

## Implementation

**The sheet was an occluder.** It is not in `AXWindows`, so `identify` names nothing for its window number,
and `Scrims.regions` stamps every window it cannot bind back to opaque. `ScrimWindow.mask` paints occluders
square on purpose.

**Keeping the sheet solid bought almost nothing, so it is veiled with its window instead.** A window emira
never adopted is skipped in the mask when the first window behind it that overlaps it is bound and contains
it wholly. That window's veil then covers the sheet. Two facts make this the honest reading rather than a
bend of the rule that a window is see-through only where the desktop is behind it:

- The window behind the sheet is itself drawn through the same photograph, so the sheet over it reads as one
  see-through window, sheet included.
- The cover already drew it that way. `SCStreamConfiguration.includeChildWindows` defaults to `true`, and a
  `desktopIndependentWindow` still of the parent carries the sheet (checked by capture). Before, the sheet
  was see-through for the length of a covered transition and turned solid when the cover came down.

*Wholly* and *first overlapping* are there for windows that are not sheets. A popup from the focused window
that hangs over its neighbour stays opaque across its whole frame instead of being veiled on part of it, and a
foreign window standing on an opaque one is unaffected.

**Rejected: rounding the occluder.** Tried first, and it works on the glass: a rounded stamp at the sheet's
radius traces it exactly. Two problems. The radius differs by window kind: measured on macOS 27 by alpha
deficit, standard windows (Ghostty, Finder, TextEdit, a standalone alert) are 17.15 pt and the sheet is
27.8 pt. And a fixed radius wrong in either direction shows: 28 pt on a 17 pt window leaves an unveiled
crescent inside its corner, 17 pt on the sheet leaves a dark rim. Constants would go stale with the OS.
Measuring is possible: `desktopIndependentWindow` on the sheet's own number returns the parent composite,
`display including:` fills the sheet's corners with the parent, but `includeChildWindows = false` isolates
it. That is a capture per window on the window server's serialized screenshot path, plus a cache and a
repaint, to keep solid a sheet nobody asked to keep solid.

## Observations

Validated live on the repro: with the other window focused, the sheet is veiled with its window and its
corners follow the window. Focusing its own window again draws it solid.

**Occluders are still square**, and `Scrims.cornerRadius` is still the hardcoded 12. A focused float standing
on an unfocused tile still leaves the tile unveiled in small square corners around it. If that proves
visible, the measured route above is the fix, and `SurfaceCache` already holds a measured radius for any
window a cover has filmed.

The test is geometric. Any never-adopted window wholly on a veiled one is veiled, sheet or not. A popover or
chrome window standing wholly on an unfocused window would now be veiled too. Whether any app puts such a
layer-0 window there has not been looked for.

Only Ghostty's sheet was checked on the glass.
