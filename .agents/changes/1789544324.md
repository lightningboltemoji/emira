# Shade the veil, so it reads alike over every window

> Change `1789544324` — 2026-09-16

## Goal

At one `unfocused-opacity`, the desktop bled through a dark window (a terminal on `#32221a`) far more
visibly than through a light one (a white web page), even with both over the same fog-and-trees
wallpaper. The number did not mean one thing across windows.

## Implementation

**It was not a luminance bug; nothing computed luminance.** Measured by capturing the scrim window, each
window, and the finished screen separately and fitting both models: the window server composites the
scrim as source-over in encoded P3 — mean error 0.26/255 against a gamma-space mix, 17.9/255 against a
linear one. So the desktop's detail adds the same encoded step to every window, and the eye does not read
it the same: over the terminal's background that step spans luminance 1.1% → 8.7% (≈8×), over white
53% → 94% (<2×). Scored as the spread of `ln(Y + flare)` over the real frosted photograph, a plain mix put
the desktop 2.75× (1% flare) to 3.3× (0.3%) more visibly on the dark window than on white, and lifted the
dark window by +9.7 L\* on average — the washed-out look.

**The fix reshapes the photograph, not the mask.** One source-over layer with one alpha per pixel can
realise any `A·W + B` per pixel with `A` and `B` chosen from the desktop, and no other shape. Plain
transparency is one member; a multiply by the desktop's luma is another, and it is the one whose
contribution scales with the window's own lightness — Weber's law — while agreeing with the mix exactly on
white. `shaded` blends the two: premultiplied `(1 − s)·D` over alpha `1 − s·luma(D)`, landing
`W·(1 − v + v·s·luma) + v·(1 − s)·D`. The model's flattest point is `s ≈ 0.75–0.8` (1.19× spread, dark shade
shift +0.2 L\*); `0.6` was chosen by eye from renders of the real windows at 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8. It is baked
in at film time beside the frost, with `vImageMatrixMultiply_ARGB8888` on the encoded bytes — one pass, no
colour management, because encoded values are what the window server blends. vImage matrices are rows of
input channel against columns of output; `ScrimShadeTests` would fail on the transpose.

**The cover draws through the same photograph.** Leaving the cover on real alpha would flash every dark
unfocused window ~10 L\* lighter for the length of every covered transition. A stand-in is now opaque, with a
sublayer holding `Scrims.backdrop(of:)` at the window's veil, cropped by `contentsRect` to the stand-in's
share of the display each frame (bottom-left unit origin on a non-flipped layer, checked with
`CALayer.render(in:)`). Rounded by its own `cornerRadius` in `.stretch`, by the clip in `.crop`.
`refreshVeils` fades that sublayer rather than `root`. This also puts the frost on the cover, which the frost
change had to leave off.

Rejected: building the same formula against the real base with standard layers — the stand-in at
`(1−v)/(1−vλ)` plus a luma-multiplied copy at `vλ` — which is exact over whatever is behind but costs a second
copy and an offscreen pass per stand-in per frame; a per-channel `multiplyBlendMode` copy, which disagrees
with the scrim's achromatic multiply on any colourful wallpaper; and a per-window veil scaled by the window's
mean luminance, which needs the window's pixels and gives a half-dark window one wrong number.

## Observations

**The cover now shows the desktop where another window is behind a stand-in** — a `stack` tile's overlap, a
role-floated dialog left behind a scrolling column. The scrim declines those regions at rest; the cover does
not carry the decline. Accepted as invisible in motion; carrying it is a mask per stand-in, per frame where
the overlap slides. Pins (the band is left clear) and hoisted floats (drawn above the cover) are not affected.

**Validated live on the scrim, not on a cover.** With the daemon on this build, the scrim's alpha along a
column of the wallpaper followed `0.25·(1 − 0.6·Y)` to ±0.004, and the composite still fit source-over to
0.37/255. The stand-in's crop orientation was checked off-screen only; a burst capture of a covered
transition was not taken because the desktop was in use.

**A stand-in's shadow is now full strength.** `root.opacity = 1 − v` used to dim it; the real desktop never
did, since the scrim stops at the window's silhouette. Not measured.

**The multiply share is achromatic**, because one alpha cannot differ per channel. On a white window over a
saturated wallpaper the tint is weaker than a plain mix by the shade share; its brightness is unchanged.

Untested on more than one display, and on a non-P3 display, where the arithmetic is in that photograph's own
encoded space.
