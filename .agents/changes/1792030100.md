# An opening window's corner is read against its own edges

> Change `1792030100` — 2026-10-14

## Goal

Clicking Zoom in the Dock during a call reopens its main window, and emira adopts it. Under `window = "crop"` the
stand-in carrying the arrival was rounded differently from the window it gave way to, so the corner visibly
changed when the cover came down.

**The radius was right. The still it was measured from was not at rest.** Instrumented through the daemon, every
reopen filmed Zoom's Login window in the first ~300 ms after it appeared, while the window server was still
animating it open. A window in that state is drawn inside a one-pixel clear border on both edges of an axis:
2632×1524 px against the settled 2628×1524, with `SCWindow.frame` reporting 1316×764 against 1314×762. A burst of
stills through that window shows the whole animation:

| ms after reopen | frame | px | radius before | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 119 | 1288×748 | 2604×1512, clear top and bottom | 22.65 | 17.00 |
| 197 | 1312×760 | 2628×1524, clear on all four | 27.20 | 17.13 |
| 298 | 1316×764 | 2632×1528, clear on all four | 27.22 | 17.16 |
| 382 on | 1314×762 | 2628×1524 | 17.15 | 17.15 |

`measuredCornerRadius` inverts the alpha deficit of a block anchored at the image's corner, so the clear column
reads as corner: 1 px down a 192 px probe is 192 px² of deficit on top of a corner's ~252, and 17.15 pt becomes
22.76. The border is on both sides at once, so the lesser corner cannot carry it. Five of seven reopens through
the daemon measured 22.76, and the stand-in was cut at that, a visibly rounder circular arc than the real corner.
With the daemon stopped, the same burst caught only rest-size stills from 73 ms on. What makes the window open
that way with the daemon running was not isolated; the measurement is fixed whatever draws the border.

## Implementation

**A clear pixel on both edges of an axis comes off before the corners are read** (`withoutBorder`). Each edge is
tested over a 16 px run at its middle, clear of every corner; a run averaging at most one alpha level counts as
clear. Only a border on *both* opposite edges is trimmed, and only one pixel of it. That is what keeps the
unpainted-edge rule intact: a band an app left unpainted is on one edge, or wider than a pixel, and still reads as
corner once one pixel comes off. `anUnpaintedEdgeDoesNotInflateTheRadius` and `unpaintedEdgesNeverUnderReport`
pass unchanged, and `aWindowFilmedOpeningMeasuresItsOwnCorner` fails on all three of its cases without the trim.

Over the 109 stills the instrumented daemon and the bursts filmed this session, every one that measured right
before measures the same (93 at 17.15), all ten mid-animation stills now read 17.00–17.16, and the faded
closing stills still answer `nil`. Through the daemon, six of six reopens measured 17.15 and the stand-in used
it. Filmed at 60 fps across one reopen, the stand-in's corner and the released window's agree to 14–16 px of a
64×64 px block at best alignment, against 153–155 before. The released window against itself is 0.

The edge reads cost 0.02–0.035 ms per still. A run down the middle half of each edge was tried first and cost
0.4–0.5 ms, more than the two corner probes together, since the cost is the run's length.

**Rejected: subtracting each corner's own edge margin inside its probe block.** It read every mid-animation still
right, but it treats an unpainted band as margin too. A band through a corner then reads as a smaller corner, the
minimum picks it, and a column hanging off both edges comes out square. **Rejected: re-rounding the stand-in from
a later film.** An arrival's batch still *is* its stand-in, and nothing films the window again before the cover
comes down.

## Observations

- **Reported as tighter, reproduced as looser.** On this machine the opening still always had the border, which
  makes the corner rounder. A still that does not fit its frame records no radius and takes
  `Reconstruction.fallbackCornerRadius`, 12 pt, which *would* be tighter. That path was not caught here: the
  window server animates an opening window with no fade (the centre of every opening still was alpha 255), so
  every opening still fitted. If tighter persists on the primary machine, that path is where to look, and a
  window's last measured radius would be a better floor than the constant.
- **The stand-in still sits a pixel off the settled window**, because it is the window as filmed at 585,45
  1316×764 and the window settles at 586,46 1314×762. The real window shows the same offset during its own open
  animation, so the cross-fade is faithful to what was filmed. Not addressed.
- **Circular against continuous.** The stand-in's clip is a circular arc, while the window's corner is a continuous
  curve with the same area. The real curve leaves the edge at ~47 px, the circle at 34. Once the radius is right
  the difference is under the 16 px residual above, and was not visible in the recordings.
- **A one-pixel unpainted band on both edges** is trimmed as a border and reads up to a point short. It needs a
  column hanging exactly half a point off both edges of the display.
- Closing stills are faded to alpha 1/255 and scaled to 98%, measure `nil`, and record nothing. Unchanged.
