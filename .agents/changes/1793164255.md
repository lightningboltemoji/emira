# A hoist's picture is the window as it is

> Change `1793164255` — 2026-10-27

## Goal

"The floating window is blank except window chrome." Reproduced with a Swing dialog that paints its content
0.8 s after it is shown: the hoist that went up showed the title bar over an empty body. A standby panel is
filmed the moment its float is on screen, and an app can put a window up before it has drawn into it — and
after that `HoistPanels.apply` filmed only a panel that was new or had changed *size*. The burial film that
`IMPLEMENTATION` and the file's own header described did not exist; change `1790137481` had noted the
discrepancy. The first photograph was the photograph for good: blank for a late painter, arbitrarily stale
for anything else. Zoom makes the second case vivid: its device-test window is one 480×310 window showing
four successive questions under its first title.

## Implementation

Three films where there was one, each through `lookAgain`, which waits for the capture plane to be idle
(`isIdle`, the predicate `CornerSurvey` already uses) so none queues in front of a cover's head batch — a
burial is usually also a cover, and that batch is the latency anyone feels:

- a **second look** `openingSettle` (1 s) after a panel is built;
- the **burial's own film**, `burialSettle` (0.3 s) after a float goes from standby to covered — long enough
  for its app to have drawn it unfocused, which is how the window now looks;
- a **refresh** of every picture that is showing when a cover comes down (`desktopSettled`, from the same
  `onCoverDismissed` that refilms the scrims' desktop), paced to one per `refreshInterval` (2 s) per window.

Rejected: a timer, for the reason the original change gave — a screenshot for a window nobody is looking at.
The refresh rides an event the user already caused, at a moment the capture plane is already idle.

## Observations

Checked with the probe: the hoist that goes up after the second look shows the dialog's content; buried and
covered again, the picture is the content as it was a moment before.

A float buried within the opening second and filmed before its app drew still shows the blank still for up
to `burialSettle` plus the wait for the capture plane. A buried float that changes while nothing on the
desktop moves is still frozen until the next cover comes down.
