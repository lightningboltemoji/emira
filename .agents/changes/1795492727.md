# Native full screen keeps its column

> Change `1795492727` — 2026-11-23

## Goal

Reported after `1795489795`: coming back from a full-screen YouTube video, the black flash was gone, and in its
place the video laid out full screen showed over the windowed page for a frame. That is the fourth symptom of one
cause. Native full screen was a departure, like a minimize: the window left the strip and its neighbours closed
up. Coming back was an arrival that had to undo that under a cover, and the exit is only seen once macOS's own
animation has started. So the cover always filmed macOS mid-animation:

- the dissolve through black (`1795488108`)
- the veil's photograph of the full-screen Space (`1795489795`)
- the base, before the desktop had faded back in
- Chromium's still, still laid out full screen

Each fix moved the flash to the next thing the cover filmed. The user chose, between keeping the column and
deferring the return until macOS's animation was over, to keep it.

## Implementation

**The model.** `World.participatesInTiling` no longer excludes a window in full screen, and `awayWindowIds` no
longer counts it. The two `windowFullScreen` handlers mark it and run the placement pass: entering writes
nothing, and leaving writes only a window that came back somewhere other than its slot. Its app restores the
frame it left, measured as `(782, 47, 998×1102)`, the slot exactly. Neither handler touches focus: the window
going full screen already holds it, and coming out it is still the window in front.

**One fact in the metrics, not a rule at every reader.** `LayoutMetrics.fullScreen` carries the set, and the
layout's id queries leave it out: `visibleWindowIds`, `sweptWindowIds`, `targetFrames`, `parkedFrames`, and so
`placedWindowIds` and `peekingFrames`. Geometry is unchanged, so the column keeps its width and nothing around it
moves. Every scope, write, park, peek and veil derives from those queries, so none of them can reach the window.
The cover scopes alone are built in four places (`scopeUnion` twice, `extendTransition`, `keptStills`). It is
where corrections, the lot and the neighbourhood already ride, for the same reason.

What this retires for full screen: the away place, the width it remembered (`1795405786`), the place it
remembered (`1795408183`), and the return cover. Minimize and ⌘H keep all three.

**The scrim plane holds through the Space switch.** The cover no longer hides the desktop's return, so the plane
had to stop misreading it. A desktop out of sight now holds its masks as well as its photograph. The two edges
come from different witnesses:

- **Going:** the core's `setDesktopShown(false)`, as before. The window server's readings change only after
  the film has gone black.
- **Coming back:** the plane's own scrim on the active Space, and the reading standing still for `settleQuiet`
  reads. Each was measured:
  - The core's word lands about 165 ms after the exit key.
  - macOS draws none of the desktop's windows until its switch is done. Ghostty, a plain probe window, a
    masked full-display bitmap, and windows at levels 0, 1 and 2, `.stationary` or not, accessory or regular
    app: all reappeared together, around 540 ms after the key.
  - A change made to a desktop window during the switch shows only at its end. Logging the scrim showed the
    reveal's cut at the core's word taking Ghostty's veil off, and the cut that restored it appearing
    527 ms after the exit was seen.
  - For about 30 ms after `isOnActiveSpace` turns true, the reading still has the returning window at full
    screen size, in front of its neighbours. A reveal on that alone dipped the veil for one to two frames.

**What is held is the mask as it last stood still** (`Scrims.steady`). The log showed a cut 13 ms before the
core's word in which Fiber, growing to full screen, had already covered Ghostty, leaving its veil an empty shape.
Holding what was painted held that empty shape. The desktop comes back as it left, so the steady mask is the
right one to hold. The film owed while away is taken at the reveal, the first moment the desktop is known back:
with no return cover, the next `desktopMayHaveChanged` could be minutes off.

`steady` is recorded where the plane sees the reading stand still, a watch or the arrival watch ending on quiet,
after its last cut. A cut no watch asked for (`recut`, `cornersMeasured`) that changes the mask starts a watch,
so every mask painted is one a stillness check will record. Without that, a watch that stood still on a lagging
reading, put right only by a recut, left `steady` empty: the next entry held Ghostty with no veil until the
reveal, about 760 ms after the exit.

Rejected:

- **Close the gap and return later.** Keeps the strip closed while away, at the price of a two-phase return:
  Fiber lands on a full-width Ghostty, which then slides narrower under a cover.
- **Extending `moving` to panes in front.** That would let the returning window's stale frame cut no veil.
  Everything passed, but the plane cannot tell a lagging reading from one that has caught up. A pane wrongly
  taken as stale in front draws a veil over a real window, such as a pin, for the length of a watch. The
  behind-only rule was deliberate.
- **Ending the hold at the core's word.** Measured: the veil came back about 150 ms after Ghostty did.

## Observations

On the desk (macOS 27.2.1, release builds, Ghostty 742 pt | Fiber 998 pt, YouTube in Fiber):

- No `beginTransition`, `capture`, `setFrame` or `park` around entering or leaving, in every round trip traced.
- Ghostty's region, dimmed, was flat from the moment it reappeared in two filmed round trips. With the hold
  released at the core's word it stepped 51 → 63 at +535 ms. With the reveal at `isOnActiveSpace` alone it
  dipped to 51 for one to two frames. The veil check read (68, 62, 56) before and after.
- Switched to the desktop while Fiber stayed full screen: Fiber's slot stood empty, Ghostty kept its column with
  the keyboard, and the core's desktop-shown answer followed focus out and back. Leaving full screen from there
  wrote nothing, and Fiber was in its slot.
- What remains in frame is macOS's. During the animation Fiber shows Chromium's full-screen layout in its
  windowed frame until Chromium relays out, about 530 ms after the exit, as it does without emira.
- What the plane's half buys, measured with `setDesktopShown` ignored (Ghostty 870 pt | Fiber 870 pt). Every
  return took Ghostty's veil off for 170–270 ms as the desktop came back (luma 49 against 60), 5 of 5. Two
  strip moves while Fiber was full screen filmed the desktop black, and Ghostty's veil read 33 against 60
  afterwards, 2 of 2. With it heeded, neither happened, 8 of 8 and 2 of 2.
- The empty `steady` was found by running a round with the hold switched off before one with it on: 4 of 4
  such entries held no veil. With the rule above, 3 of 3 held the right one.

Not exercised:

- Safari's element full screen. Its player window is adopted as a tile before it goes full screen (`1795405786`),
  so it now keeps a column while it plays, and closing it at the end closes the column under a cover.
- Focusing a full-screen window from the desktop by emira's verbs. The strip may scroll to frame its column
  under a cover while macOS switches Space.
- A second display, and the hoist panels through a Space switch.
- A column the strip moved while its window was away is written once as the window lands, during macOS's
  animation. Covered by a test, not filmed.

The reveal waits for a still reading under a bound (`returnLimit`, 1.5 s). A reading that stood still for four
reads before catching up would be cut against; the lag measured was two reads.
