# The desktop is not filmed while a full-screen Space stands over it

> Change `1795489795` — 2026-11-23

## Goal

Reported: after a window goes full screen and comes back, the dimmed windows are dimmed toward black
instead of the wallpaper, until windows are swapped. Measured on the desk with Fiber and Ghostty (Ghostty
dimmed, Fiber focused): a 1 s round trip took Ghostty's mean colour from (68, 62, 56) to (28, 21, 17). The
same patch of a dimmed window read (76, 73, 55) before the round trip and a neutral (21, 21, 22) after.

The veil's photograph was filmed with the full-screen window's Space in front. Going full screen moves the
strip under a cover. When that cover comes down, about 0.8 s after the key, `desktopMayHaveChanged`
refilms, and the display minus its windows is black there: a probe mirroring `DesktopCapturer` read
(0, 0, 0) for as long as Fiber was full screen. Back on the desktop, the refilm after the return cover is
inside `desktopMaxAge` of the black one whenever the trip is short, so the black stands until a cover at
least 2 s later, such as a swap. On a longer trip it is replaced, but only after the return cover has drawn
its stand-ins' veils through it, and the replacement lands just after that cover comes down. That fits the
step lighter about 90 ms after the cover which `1795488108` left uninvestigated, and filmed on this change,
Ghostty's region is flat after the crossfade.

## Implementation

**The core says which desktops are out of sight**: `State.desktopsUnderFullScreen`, kept as
`unshownDesktops` and sent on a change as `Effect.setDesktopShown`, ahead of the batch's `setScrims`. A
display is out of sight when the window last focused on it is in native full screen. Going full screen keeps
the keyboard (`Event.windowFullScreen`), so this holds from the event onward, and focus landing on a desktop
window is what says the user has left the Space.

It has to be the core, because the window server says so late. Sampled every 30 ms through two round trips,
the film began darkening 280–435 ms after the key and was black from 536–690 ms. Ghostty leaving the window
list, Finder's desktop window leaving it, and a scrim-like window's `isOnActiveSpace` all changed at
632–801 ms. The core's event lands at about 190 ms. Under the default transition the entry cover came down
about 95 ms after the window server caught up. Under `snap` it comes down well before, so gating on
`isOnActiveSpace` alone would have let that film through.

**The plane holds, turns away, and owes.** While a display is out of sight, `refilm` takes no film, the
throttle's bypasses included. Going out of sight bumps the generation, so a film in flight is dropped. It
also marks the display `owed`: the first `desktopMayHaveChanged` after it is back films whatever the
throttle says, because a radius change or a rebuilt surface may have been turned away meanwhile, and the
wallpaper may have changed. Coming back does not film at once. The core hears of the exit about 165 ms after
the key, and the film read part-dark until about 190 ms.

Rejected:
- `isOnActiveSpace`, or the window list, as the whole test: right on the way back, late on the way in.
- Emptying the veil set while the desktop is out of sight: the stand-ins read their veils from the plane,
  so Ghostty would brighten under the entry cover and dim again after the exit.
- Judging the film by its pixels: a black wallpaper is a wallpaper.
- Observing `activeSpaceDidChangeNotification`, which the `Scrims` header claimed was done and never was. It
  names no display, and it arrives with the window server's other readings, after the film has gone black.

The header and `desktopMayHaveChanged`'s doc now list only the triggers that exist.

## Observations

On the desk (macOS 27.2.1, release build, Ghostty 742 pt | Fiber 998 pt): Ghostty read (68, 62, 56) before
and after round trips of 0.6, 1 and 4 s, against (28, 21, 17) after 1 s on the build before. The trace showed
`setDesktopShown(false)` on each entry and `(true)` on each exit. On the 0.6 s trip the entry cover came down
55 ms before the exit, while out of sight, and was turned away.

Not settled:

- The return cover is still filmed mid-animation. Its base is taken at the exit, while macOS is still
  fading the desktop in, so for about half a second the gaps and Ghostty's translucent background read darker
  (46, 33, 28 against 74, 64, 57 once live) and then snap right at the crossfade. That predates this change,
  which does not touch the base.
- A swipe back to the desktop with no focus landing there keeps the display out of sight, so its photograph
  is held, not black, until focus arrives.
- Not exercised on a second display. Each display's answer comes from the window last focused on it.
- By reading, not observed: a daemon started while a full-screen Space is in front orders its scrim windows
  in on that Space, since they belong to the Space they were ordered in on. This does not address it.
