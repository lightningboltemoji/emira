# A cover one level short of opaque

> Change `1795488108` — 2026-11-23

## Goal

Reported: taking a YouTube video full screen in Fiber and back flashes black. Filmed at 60 fps against
`EMIRA_TRACE` (Ghostty at 742 pt, Fiber at 998 pt), the black is the cover's dissolve. The frame darkens from the
first frame of the 220 ms fade, reaches black, and cuts to the desktop on `crossfadeDone`. With
`animation.transition = off` the same exit slides from 124 to 84 mean luma with no dip. The build before
`1795408183` flashes the same way, so the return-to-slot work did not cause it.

The cause is `Overlay.raisedAlpha = 0.999`. The window server treats a window there as opaque and composites
nothing beneath it, so fading the cover fades it over black. A standalone window with the overlay's settings
(borderless, opaque, `.floating`, the same collection behaviour) raised and faded on emira's timings showed
it, with no emira cover involved:

| raised to          | fade, 220 ms                    | runs |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- | ---- |
| 0.999              | through black, then a cut       | 7    |
| 0.998, 0.997, 0.996 | clean, never below the desktop | 3    |
| 0.99               | clean                           | 1    |

The 0.999 runs include ones with no full screen at all, a 50 ms hold, and 150 pt of the display left uncovered.
The last means this is not apps throttled for being hidden. The full-screen exit is only where it shows
worst. On HEAD an ordinary `move-window` dissolve sat frozen for its 220 ms, and then the video area was black
for about 100 ms after the cover dropped, which is the artifact `1785549810` raised the cover to 0.999 to prevent.

## Implementation

**`raisedAlpha = 254/255`.** The cutoff lies between 0.998 and 0.999. That is where an 8-bit alpha rounds from
254 to 255, so the value is put a whole level under it rather than at 0.998, which is 254.49 and sits a
hundredth of a level from the boundary. The defect was a value on the wrong side of a line nobody had measured,
so the replacement is not placed next to that line either.

A second line sits near 1, and it is not the same one. A probe window under a full-display cover reads
`occlusionState` `.occluded` at 1.0 and `.visible` at 0.999, 0.998, 254/255 and 0.99. That is what
`1785549810` and `1791953609` measured, and it still holds. 0.999 clears occlusion and not the compositor.
The comments and `IMPLEMENTATION.md` had folded the two into "any alpha under 1".

**The fence compares at `Float`.** The server keeps alpha as a `Float` and `kCGWindowAlpha` reports that
value back: 254/255 is read back as 0.99607843160629272, above the `Double` that was set. The comparison passed
by the luck of rounding up, for 0.999 as well. Compared at the server's precision, it no longer depends on
which way the rounding goes.

**The tarp keeps 0.999.** Its alpha is a gate flipped at once, never a fade, so whatever the compositor leaves
undrawn beneath it is never shown through it, and occlusion is all it has to clear. Its comments now give that
reason instead of borrowing the cover's.

Rejected: fading the layer tree instead of the window, which `1785549810` already found fades to black. Rejected:
going lower than one level, which buys nothing and lets more of the windows teleporting underneath through.

## Observations

On the desk (macOS 27.2.1, built-in display, release builds), before and after:

- Full-screen exit, HEAD: black in 2 of 2 rounds in this layout, and 1 of 1 on the parent commit. With
  this change: clean in 3 of 3, plus 2 of 2 at 0.998. The crossfade stays inside the range of the
  frames either side of it.
- Ordinary moves, with the video playing: flat through the dissolve and after it in 4 of 4. HEAD's ended with a
  17-point drop and a dark video plane.
- The cover-up check still answers on its first ask: the cover was up 55–75 ms after the exit was seen, against
  42–70 ms on HEAD.

Not settled:

- The 8-bit rounding is inferred from where the cutoff lies; the server's rule was not read. Neither is when
  0.999 stopped clearing it. `1785549810` filmed flat dissolves at 0.999 in July.
- At 0.999 the result varied. One HEAD round with Fiber first in the strip at 1320 pt dissolved cleanly, and the
  ordinary move froze instead of darkening. What decides between the three was not found.
- Whether the one level of transparency is ever visible while windows teleport under a cover was not looked for.
- The tarp was not filmed with windows parked under it; its 0.999 rests on the occlusion probe and on its
  never fading.
- Fiber's still is filmed mid-exit, while Chromium still lays the page out full screen, so the dissolve now
  visibly blends the large player into the page. That was always the cover's content; the black hid it.
- About 90 ms after a full-screen exit's cover comes down, the unfocused window's veil steps lighter. HEAD does
  the same. Not investigated.
- No test: the threshold is the window server's, and nothing in the suite composites.
