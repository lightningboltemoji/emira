# The tarp: parked windows drawn over

> Change `1791953609` — 2026-10-13

## Goal

A parked window cannot leave the screen, so it waits as a nub in the lot's corner, a single point of the
window poking in from the right edge. Against a dark wallpaper a light window's nub is a visible bright line.
Measured on the development display, the nub is 2 device px of the window's own colour, 40 pt tall plus 8 per
ordinal, with a near-black rim 1 device px wide just outside its frame. Its unfocused shadow takes a 224
wallpaper to 150 at the edge and fades over about 16 pt left and 20 pt above. On a dark wallpaper only the nub
shows. On a light one the rim and the shadow are a smudge, so a mask of the nub alone would trade a dark line
for a bright one. The goal is to hide all of it.

## Implementation

**What is drawn is the corner with nothing parked in it**: the desktop photograph, with the shadow of every
window that is not parked drawn back in as the cover's stand-ins draw theirs, and those windows cut out. The
photograph cannot carry the shadows itself. ScreenCaptureKit display captures drop every window's shadow, even
with `ignoreShadowsDisplay = false`, while `screencapture` keeps them. Before building it, the patch was
prototyped as a separate process, a probe window tiled beside it. On the corner with no nubs, compared against
the real desktop:

| Neighbour | Mean error | p99 | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| focused (σ 20) | 0.74 | 2 | 3 |
| unfocused (σ 8) | 0.87 | 3 | 3 |

That is 8-bit, worst channel, over the whole gap, after the three fixes below. Before them the maxima were 44
to 74, all of it on the neighbour's outline.

- **The hole reaches 1.25 pt past a neighbour's frame** (`TarpWindow.rim`). macOS draws the dark rim
  0.5–1.0 pt outside the frame along the corner's diagonal. A hole at the frame painted over it: an error of
  44 along every edge, and 74 at the corner arc with 0.5 pt. At 1.25 pt nothing on the arc differs.
- **The patch is whole points.** A rect at 1768.5 became a window at 1768, and everything inside it shifted
  one device pixel: the photograph stepped against a grainy wallpaper and the hole covered the rim.
- **Interior edges fade over 10 pt** (`TarpWindow.feather`). With three nubs stacked, their shadows still
  leave 1–2 levels at 31 pt. A hard edge there measured a step of up to 6 against a baseline of 3. Feathered,
  it reads at the baseline.

**The core names the parked windows and their slots; the shell trusts the window server for where they
stand.** `writeTruthPlane` now records `World.parkedSlots` beside `placedOnScreen`, held displays keeping
theirs. `State.tarp` carries the slots, the lot (so the shell has no second opinion about where it is) and the
focused window, keyed by the lot's display. `TarpCut.cut` conceals a pane only where its origin is within
`Tolerance.identity` of its slot, so a park still landing, or one an app refused, is a neighbour rather than
painted over in view. `Effect.setTarp` rides `settleScrims`: the same gate, the same re-send on a settled
desktop, and empty while a window is in the hand.

**It lives in the scrim plane, as a second surface.** The settle watch, the arrival watch, the stack-change
recut, the cover handshake and the photograph were all already there. A standalone window would have
duplicated them, and its hardest part is the scrims' too: stopping at windows that are not desktop. The tarp
is cut first and the veils then skip what it conceals, the way they skip a departing window.

**Three bugs the live runs found**, each fixed and pinned by a test that fails without the fix:

- **The key shadow on a parked window.** A switch to an empty workspace leaves macOS's key window where it was,
  parked, and emira's focus `nil`. Its σ 20 shadow overran a patch padded for σ 8, leaving a band dipping
  from 190 to 182 at the patch's edge. Fix: pad for the key shadow when the focused window is unnamed or
  parked.
- **A nub on the first frame of a cover's dismissal.** Under a cover the tarp is cut once, while the parks are
  still landing, and the settle watch's first read comes an interval after the fade starts. Filmed at 60 fps,
  one frame in four switches showed a parked window's edge and rim. Fix: `setCovered(false)` recuts at once.
  Nine switches after the fix showed no nub on any frame.
- **A white sliver where a veiled column peeks past a nub behind it.** The veil declined over the opaque nub,
  leaving the column unveiled for 1 pt × 8 pt at the display's edge. This predates the tarp: any parked
  window behind a veiled one did it. Fix: concealed panes leave the veil walk.

**The window holds `alpha 0.999`.** A probe window parked under a tarp-shaped window read `.occluded` at alpha
1, opaque or not, and `.visible` at 0.999. That is the throttling parking is designed to avoid.

**The cover's base cuts what the tarp conceals** (`BaseRequest.concealed`), or every transition would show the
nubs the live desktop hides.

**The strip is filmed untouched, in the same film as the scrims' photograph**, which now takes an optional
blur and an optional strip (`DesktopFilm`). With only a tarp it films no veiled photograph, so
`backdrop(of:)` stays `nil` and covers build no veil layers.

Rejected:

- **A 2 px strip of our own**, the starting idea. It leaves the rim and the shadow, which are the visible
  part on a light wallpaper. It would also paint wallpaper over a column peeking through the corner, which
  the scrims' cut handles.
- **Silhouette-only masking.** On a light wallpaper it paints a bright line at the edge of the shadow's smudge.
- **Patching only where no neighbour's shadow reaches.** With 20 pt outer gaps the right column's key
  shadow, reaching 77 pt, covers the corner almost always.
- **A photograph minus the parked windows, refilmed as needed.** It has no shadows to offer, and a refilm on
  every focus change is the capture the scrims refuse to pay.

## Observations

- **Focus changes** were timed with `CGDisplayCreateImageForRect` (dlsym'd; about 12 ms a frame, and unlike
  ScreenCaptureKit it includes shadows), sampling inside and outside the patch. macOS steps a window's shadow
  between key and not, with no animation. The tarp's step lands 7–12 ms after the change and the real one at
  19–22 ms, so the two are at most a frame apart.
- **A hand lifts the tarp**, so nubs show for the length of a drag or a hand resize. Keeping it unless the
  hand's window nears the corner is the obvious refinement.
- **A nub in front of a column peeking through the corner stays visible.** The column's pixels are not ours
  to paint. A nub behind one shows through the column's rim for a single device row, inside the hole's
  1.25 pt.
- **The strip is as fresh as the scrims' photograph**: refilmed after a cover comes down, at most every
  `desktopMaxAge`. A wallpaper change, or a dynamic wallpaper's drift, with no transition after it leaves the
  corner showing the old one. Widgets and icons in the corner are frozen the same way.
- **A foreign window moving into the corner** is painted over until the next recut, as a scrim's is.
- **`aCrossingIntoAFloatScrollsNothing`** asserted the effects were exactly `[.focus]`. With windows parked, a
  focus change now also re-sends the tarp, so the test filters it out.
- While driving probe windows, emira never tiled a second window of the same app within 8 s, with or without
  a bundle id; the first tiled at once. Not investigated.
