# A desktop film retakes a window that arrived during it

> Change `1793164259` — 2026-10-27

## Goal

The scrims' photograph is the display with every window taken out of it, and which windows to take out is
read a moment *before* the screenshot. A window ordered in between the two is in the photograph — behind every
veil until the next film, and no focus change brings one. The film is taken when a cover comes down, which is
when Zoom puts up its floating video window: focus has just left the meeting.

Measured with a magenta borderless window ordered in and out every 50 ms against emira's own film code: 1 of
60 films in one run, 53 of 200 in another, held the window.

## Implementation

`DesktopCapturer` reads the window list again after the shot. A window at layer ≥ 0 on screen that the
exclusion did not name means the film may hold it, so it is taken again against the newer list, up to three
attempts (`desktopAttempts`), and otherwise given up — a failed film already leaves the photograph held
standing, and already paces the next attempt. One extra content read per film, about 10 ms on a quiet desktop.

## Observations

Against the same flicker: 200 films, none kept with the window in it; 87 rejected, 53 of which did hold it — 34
clean films paid a retake, which only a window flickering at 20 Hz costs. The cover's base has the same race
and is left alone: a window that arrived is genuinely on the desktop, and the base lives for one transition.
