# A float leaving the glass takes its hole in the veil with it

> Change `1790921572` — 2026-10-01

## Goal

The other end of `1790920494`. A float closed over a veiled column left a float-sized hole in the veil — the
column under it unveiled — long after the window was gone.

## Implementation

**Nothing recut the mask until the sweep.** Measured on the same harness, a third process sampling the window
list against the daemon's trace: a closed float's `windowDestroyed` reached the core 15–26 ms after the close
and produced `setHoists([])` and nothing else. The window faded out over ~260 ms, and the hole stood until the
reconciliation sweep's `onStackChanged` recut it — 2.3 s after the close. A minimized float was worse: the
genie runs ~556 ms and `windowMinimized` only lands after it (600 ms), and the sweep came at 4.1 s. The core
sends a set when its bindings change or when the desktop settles under writes, and a float moves neither: it
was never veiled, only cut around.

**`Engine.restacked` re-sends the set, as a cut, when a window off the layout reaches or leaves the glass** —
`World.isOnScreen` turning over across a `windowCreated`, `windowDestroyed`, `windowMinimized`,
`windowDeminimized` or `windowShown`, on the display under the window's centre. It joins `windowSelfPlaced` as
a re-send for a desktop that moved under an unchanged set. In the core rather than as another shell fan-out
because the core knows of these windows, and *the core says when to read* is the plane's rule; the shell's
fan-outs are for what the core cannot know (a window never managed, one not yet adopted).

**No polling.** A closing window is already in `WindowRegistry.departedNumbers` when its `windowDestroyed` is
reduced, and the mask skips a departed pane, so one recut is right at once — the veil closes over the window's
fade, which is the departed rule's standing choice. A minimized window is off the glass before AX says so.
Live, after: the hole closed 27 ms after a close (the core heard it at 15 ms) and 599 ms after a minimize,
41 ms after the genie left the list — whichever of `windowShown(false)` and `windowMinimized` came first; the
second, finding the window already off the glass, sends nothing.

**It replaces the scan fan-out `1790920494` added** (`WorldWatcher` firing `onStackChanged` on adoption): a
float's adoption is a window off the layout reaching the glass, and a tiled one's transition sends a set of its
own. Checked for what else it bought: a tab succession, where the new tab's window stands on the old one's
rectangle, so the mask comes out the same.

## Observations

- A float coming back from the Dock genies in under the veil of whatever it lands on, ~0.5 s: AX posts
  `windowDeminimized` when the genie ends, and nothing earlier can arm a watch. It is cut out the moment that
  lands — measured before this change, by the focus report right behind it, which changed the set anyway.
- A window emira never managed closing over a veiled one still waits for the sweep, as before: no notification
  reaches us for it.
- The recut is a cut, so the veil is at full strength over the window for the rest of its fade. A dissolve
  would be the core's word to give if that reads as a step; it was not changed here.
